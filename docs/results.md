### Non-Significant Associations

The following outcomes showed no evidence of causal association:
- Gestational diabetes
- Preeclampsia
- Most birthweight categories
- Caesarean section outcomes
- Most neonatal outcomes

### Clinical Implications

1. **Fertility counseling**: Women with endometriosis should be informed about increased infertility risk
2. **Pregnancy monitoring**: Enhanced surveillance for placental complications in endometriosis patients
3. **Preterm birth prevention**: Consideration of PROM risk in management strategies

### Comparison with Observational Evidence

Our MR findings support observational studies showing:
- Increased infertility rates in endometriosis
- Higher prevalence of placental disorders
- Elevated preterm birth risk

### Limitations of Current Results

- Effect sizes may not capture non-linear relationships
- Results specific to European ancestry
- Potential for residual pleiotropy in some associations

### Future Directions

1. **Replication**: Validation in independent cohorts
2. **Mechanistic studies**: Investigation of biological pathways
3. **Multi-ancestry analysis**: Extension to diverse populations
4. **Fetal genetic effects**: Separate maternal vs. fetal contributions
